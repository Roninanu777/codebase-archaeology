## Failure-mode migration, measured

The two known_gap cases changed character:

- Before tier-2: rationale chunks did not exist. Abstention-honesty was the
  only correct behavior.
- After tier-2: pr:19108 discussion carries 11.7KB of lanes rationale;
  pr:17331 carries 4.3KB on the entry-point split. The chunks exist but rank
  outside top-50 for their questions against 41k competitors.

That is progress you can point at: the remaining problem is ranking, not data.
DESIGN.md §6 prescribed the fix before we needed it — a cross-encoder rerank
stage between RRF fusion and liveness scoring. That stage is now the top
priority for retrieval quality.

Honest limits, kept visible:
- lanes/createRoot rationale chunks remain unreachable in *any* candidate
  pool (dense ranks 2359/38k; sparse 1465/1166 of 41k) — first-stage recall,
  not reranking; needs query expansion or a stronger first-stage embedder.
- Cross-encoders truncate at 512 tokens, so long discussions are judged
  largely by their openings. Likely explains the context-API regression vs
  fused-only; chunk-level summarization is the candidate fix.
- Model choice measured, not assumed: ms-marco-MiniLM was worse overall
  (sum-of-ranks 20 vs 15); env `ARCHAEOLOGY_RERANKER_MODEL` keeps A/B open.

## Addendum 2 - sub-chunking + a correction (same day)

Long discussions are now split into comment-aligned bins (<=1600 chars,
`#N` suffixed source ids); corpus grew 41,316 -> **54,691 chunks** and every
discussion bin now fits inside the cross-encoder's 512-token window.

**Correction to Addendum 1**: I claimed pr:19108 carried "11.7KB of lanes
rationale". That was wrong — I measured chunk *length*, never read the
*content*. Verified today: its discussion is a devtools-debugging thread
with zero "lane" mentions; #17331 likewise never says createRoot or render.
The lanes/createRoot rationale does not exist anywhere in facebook/react PR
discussions — it lives in rfcs/blogs/talks. The known_gap classification was
correct all along; case notes now record VERIFIED ABSENT with evidence.

What sub-chunking actually bought, measured:
- Retrieval holds **3/3 at 54.7k chunks** (ranks softened r2/r4/r14 -> r5/r5/r15,
  expected as corpus doubled under fixed k)
- Discussion bins are rerank-truncation-safe; commit/PR-body behavior unchanged
- The remaining recall ceiling on gap-style questions is a *corpus* boundary,
  not a ranking one: no embedder or reranker can surface text that was never
  written down there
